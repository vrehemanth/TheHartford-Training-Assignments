Some Types of Insurance

Health Insurance

Health insurance covers hospitalization, medical treatment, surgeries, medicines, emergencies and checkups, reducing financial burden during illness.

Life Insurance

Life insurance provides financial support to family after policyholder’s death, helping manage expenses, debts, education needs and ensuring long-term security.

Vehicle Insurance

Vehicle insurance protects cars or bikes against accidents, theft, fire, damages and third-party liabilities, covering repair costs and legal responsibilities.

Property Insurance

Property insurance safeguards homes and belongings from fire, theft, disasters, accidents and damage, helping owners recover losses and rebuild safely.

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance covers trip cancellations, delays, lost baggage, passport loss, medical emergencies and accidents during travel, ensuring safe, worry-free journeys.

Commercial Insurance

Business insurance protects companies from financial losses due to property damage, lawsuits, employee risks, theft, disasters and severe business interruptions.
